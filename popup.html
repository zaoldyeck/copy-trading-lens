Copy Trading Lens

Live copy-trader risk analysis

How to use

Open a Binance or OKX lead-trader detail page. The analysis panel appears automatically and computes risk locally from data visible to your current browser session.

What it checks

  • Martingale-like adverse adds and long loss holds
  • Loss-period capital injections and current floating-loss risk
  • Payoff ratio, expectancy, sample size, and MDD
  • Slippage and copyability risk in high-frequency micro-profit strategies

Privacy

No collection, upload, storage of cookies, headers, API keys, or account data. There is no backend service.
